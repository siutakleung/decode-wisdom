# Chapter 1: First Exploration of Knowledge and Wisdom

*Explaining the Purpose of Knowledge and Wisdom.*

## 1.1 Two Most Important Functions of Wisdom

Stop for a moment. Take five minutes to think: what do you believe are the two most important functions of wisdom?

Not three — only two. The two capabilities that best summarize and express your understanding of wisdom.

Continue reading only after you have thought it through.

## 1.2 Two Most Important Functions of Knowledge

Similarly, take five minutes to think: what do you believe are the two most important functions of knowledge?

The two capabilities that best summarize and express your understanding of knowledge.

Wait — this most fundamental question will be answered later!
