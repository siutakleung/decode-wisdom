# Chapter 1: First Exploration of Knowledge and Wisdom

*Explaining the purpose of knowledge and wisdom.*

## 1.1 Two Most Important Functions of Wisdom

Stop for a moment. Take five minutes to think: what do you think are the two most important functions of wisdom?

Not three — only two. The two abilities that best sum up and show what wisdom means to you.

Keep reading only after you have thought about it.

## 1.2 Two Most Important Functions of Knowledge

In the same way, take five minutes to think: what do you think are the two most important functions of knowledge?

The two abilities that best sum up and show what knowledge means to you.

Wait — this most basic question will be answered later!
